How SETU works

Step 1 of 6

01 · Supervisor / Field engineer

Record what happened on site.

A supervisor writes a natural-language update with site context. SETU keeps the original observation as the evidence source.

SETU's interpretation layer extracts useful signals while keeping the original wording available for human verification.

Why this matters

A free-form note can be understood as a structured progress signal, without forcing supervisors to identify Primavera activity IDs.

Interpretation can be incomplete or ambiguous. SETU should surface uncertainties rather than presenting every extraction as certain.

03 · Schedule linking

Connect evidence to a planned activity.

The matching layer suggests a schedule activity based on description, discipline and field context. The link is a recommendation, not an automatic baseline edit.

04 · Validation

Check context before accepting a link.

SETU keeps validation distinct from matching. Schedule context and evidence constraints must be checked before a planner confirms a decision.

Example checks Human review

Verify the referenced activity exists in the schedule index.

Compare discipline, location and planned window.

Check report revision and unresolved clarification.

Important safeguard

A blocked or uncertain validation is not a pass. The planner must see the real warnings and decide through the governed workflow.

These are illustrative checks, not live database validation results.

05 · Human planner decision

A planner makes the accountable decision.

The authenticated Planner workspace provides review, clarification and remapping actions. AI suggestions never substitute for a human authorization decision.

Why this is different

The report stays traceable. Uncertain activity links are not silently approved, and a follow-up from the site can clarify the actual progress.

No production approval or clarification action is executed here.

06 · Traceability

Follow the evidence. See why it matters.

SETU's audit-first design associates reports, proposed links, human decisions and follow-up evidence so reviewers can reconstruct the process.

Illustrative audit trail Sample

Next action

Supervisor clarifies; planner records a final decision through SETU.

What the evaluator should take away

Field observations have a durable context.

Schedule linking is explainable and reviewable.

Human decisions are distinguishable from AI suggestions.

No automatic Primavera baseline writeback.
