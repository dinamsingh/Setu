The translation gap

The work happened.
The schedule needs context.

A site note says “spool fit-up finished.” A plan needs an activity, a location and evidence. SETU makes that connection visible so the planner can decide.

Evidence, not promises

What is implemented today?

Prototype source reviewed at commit 120b5e7 · 23 October 2026. Repository implementation does not prove every feature is configured in the hosted app.

Public demo
Six static, reproducible stages; synthetic fixtures and sample audit. No engine or database connection.
Matching & validation
Implemented in hybrid matcher and six validation checks. Aliases, semantic and fuzzy similarity, discipline and location evidence are combined.
Human control & security
Repository includes authenticated Supervisor and Planner workflows, accept/reject/remap review and audit/export. Supabase Auth, role checks and RLS protect the real app. The public demo has no access to those records.
Hosted availability
Live prototype (sign-in required) ↗: public shell reachable; authenticated operations and backend health are not verified here.
Innovation
Connect field terminology to schedule context, expose competing candidates and validation evidence, and retain planner corrections as reviewable institutional memory.
Applicability & limits
Designed for oil and gas construction progress review against imported P6 schedules. Requires project-specific terminology, reliable observations and planner judgment. No live P6 write-back, OIL pilot, autonomous approval or production accuracy is claimed.
